# Brag Plan: Tensi Investor Prep

## What is this app?
A self-built flashcard trainer with 85 real, hard investor questions (with model answers and "why they ask this" notes) that a founder built to drill themselves before their May 7 fundraise.

## The angle
This isn't a generic productivity app — it's a founder grilling themselves with the exact questions a VC will throw at them ("What if I want 20% equity?", "What stops Procore from building this?") before walking into the room. The video should feel like watching someone prep for the biggest meeting of their life: the countdown, the flip-card drilling, the progress bar climbing toward "ready." It's earnest and high-stakes, not jokey — the tension is real because the questions are real.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The splash screen, recreated exactly: dark teal background, "ten**si**" logotype (amber "si") scaling in, with "Investor Prep" and "May 7 · 85 Questions" settling underneath. The date is the hook — it puts a deadline on screen before anything else.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The flip card: a real hard question on the front ("What if I want 20% equity?" — category "Equity & Deal", difficulty "Hard"), a simulated tap/flip reveals the back with the model answer and a "Why They Ask This" callout.
- Category tabs sliding across (Market, Product & AI, Team, Financials, Equity & Deal, Risk) — showing the breadth of what's being drilled.
- The progress bar and "practised" counter climbing — the mechanic that makes this feel like training, not reading.

## Outro / punchline
The app's own completion screen, recreated: checkmark, "You're Ready." and "Every question practised. May 7 is yours." Hold on the wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, exactly as the app works:
1. **Entry:** Splash screen — "Investor Prep · May 7 · 85 Questions."
2. **Key action:** Swipe/flip through a hard question by category, reveal the answer and the reasoning behind it, mark it practised.
3. **Result:** Progress bar fills, done-counter climbs, "You're Ready" screen lands.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: the quiet, focused ritual of prepping for the meeting that decides whether the round closes.
- Interpretation: restraint over hype. Few scenes, longer holds, confident pacing. No jokes, no exclamation points — the stakes carry the energy. Type stays mixed-case, generous spacing, nothing aggressive.

Audio-coupled idea: a soft tick/click on the first and last tab landing; counter tick synced loosely to the beat grid, not every number.
Music: steady bed continues; category reveal biased toward the ~13.11s strong cue.

Audio-coupled idea: one success-style accent (bell/soft-impact family) as the checkmark and headline land, biased toward the ~18.56s strong cue; music holds and fades under the final wordmark hold.
